# Ash — Cabinet of Living Records

## Story — The Archivist and the Unfinished Page
An archivist found a page with a beautiful conclusion and no references. She did not burn it or file it as truth. She placed it in a cabinet marked “promising, unverified,” then invited others to bring sources. Years later, the page had become more useful because its uncertainty had been preserved.

## Art direction — Cabinet of evidence
A museum cabinet with transparent drawers labeled SOURCE, CLAIM, EVIDENCE, COUNTERPOINT, and REVIEW. One drawer remains intentionally open to show that the archive welcomes correction.

## Music sketch — “Unfinished Page” (original)
A sparse piano phrase in G minor: G–D–Bb–A | G–F–D–G. Repeat once, then leave the last measure silent. The silence represents evidence not yet found.

## Curation rules
No claim graduates by repetition. Record supporting and contrary evidence, source quality, uncertainty, and reviewer decisions. Keep personal and sensitive records out of public collections.
